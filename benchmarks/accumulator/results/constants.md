integer arithmetic only: hex-float parsing is exact, scaling by `2^64`
and `2^128` is exact integer/rational, and rounding is explicit
round-nearest-even.  No `long double` and no `double` representation of
`2^64` or `2^128` is used.

## Zero low lanes

`X0_128_lo` and `D_128_lo` are both exactly zero. `x0` and `Delta` are
IEEE doubles, so their significands occupy at most 53 bits and their
exact scalings by `2^128` have no bits below `2^-64`. The FX128 low lane
therefore stays zero for every iteration and the carry out of the low
lane is always zero. The carry is still generated and consumed on every
iteration as section 2.3 requires - the sequence is branchless, so its
cost does not depend on the carry value - but FX128 carries no
information that FX64 does not. See summary.md sections 7 and 10.
